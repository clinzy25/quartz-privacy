# Passport Cards
Passport cards are often an acceptable substitute for ID.
## Applying for a passport card
If you do not have a passport, you can apply for a normal passport and a passport card together. During the process, look for an option to order a passport card as well.

If you already have a passport but no passport card, you use [form DS-82](https://eforms.state.gov/Forms/ds82_pdf.PDF).
- Fill out the form
- Include your existing passport
- Include the fee listed [here](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/passports/need-passport/card.html)
- Include a short letter explaining that you are ordering a card and do not cancel the passport book.
- Mail to address denoted in the HOW TO SUBMIT THIS FORM section, use priority mail flat envelope, which is tracked and insured.


- Always refuse ID scanning. If they insist, cite US code title 18, part 1, chapter 33, section 701. If they still insist, move on.
- Sometimes you must submit ID through and online portal. If you do, modify it by redacting the image, and adding text like "Scanned image created for X company. DO NOT USE FOR IDENTITY VERIFICATION", that would identify the company who leaked your info, and discourage any further use of the document.
